Miraculous Season 7 Global Premiere Schedule Revealed!

M
By Miraculous Network
Miraculous News Desk
📅 Published: September 24, 2026  •  ⏱️ 3 min read

Season 7 global premiere schedule shown in the provided announcement.

Pound it, Miraculous fans! The Season 7 premiere schedule is here! A new global premiere schedule shows when the first eight episodes are currently listed to air across Disney Channel EMEA and Disney Channel CEE.

The rollout begins in November 2026, with Disney Channel EMEA scheduled to launch the season with a special double-episode premiere before continuing with Episode 3 the following week. Disney Channel CEE then follows with Episodes 4 through 8 across late November and early December.

📢 Important Schedule Note The dates in this article are based on the schedule shown in the provided announcement. Broadcast schedules can change, and release dates may differ between territories.

🌍 Disney Channel EMEA Premiere Dates

The EMEA rollout currently lists the first three episodes. The season begins with Episodes 1 and 2 airing together on November 14, followed by Episode 3 one week later.

  • November 14, 2026: Episode 1 & Episode 2
  • November 21, 2026: Episode 3

🌎 Disney Channel CEE Premiere Dates

The CEE schedule is considerably more packed, with five episodes currently listed between November 26 and December 2.

  • November 26, 2026: Episode 4
  • November 27, 2026: Episode 5
  • November 30, 2026: Episode 6
  • December 1, 2026: Episode 7
  • December 2, 2026: Episode 8

📸 The Announcement

The schedule image featured above is the announcement currently being used for this release-date update. It lists the EMEA and CEE dates and confirms the first eight episodes included in the schedule.

🐞 What This Means for Season 7

With the first eight episodes now attached to specific dates in these two regions, fans have a clearer timeline for the beginning of Season 7. The release pattern is different between EMEA and CEE, so viewers should check the schedule for their own territory before planning their watch dates.

As more regional premiere information becomes available, this article can be updated with additional broadcasters and dates.

💖 Keep Your Calendar Ready!

The first listed premiere is November 14, 2026, when Disney Channel EMEA is scheduled to air Episodes 1 and 2. The currently listed rollout then continues through December 2, 2026, with Episode 8 on Disney Channel CEE.
